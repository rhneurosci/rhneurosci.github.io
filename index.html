Focusing on brain-inspired information processing and biomimetic intelligence, we use fruit-fly behavior and human cognitive tasks as two major entry points, integrating behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and AI methods to study perception, decision-making, and navigation in complex environments. We also combine EEG, eye tracking, and gamified paradigms to investigate the neural mechanisms of attention, working memory, executive control, and decision-making.

研究概述 研究概述 Research Overview

本课题组以果蝇行为研究与人类认知任务研究为主要切入点，关注复杂环境中的感知编码、行为选择与决策机制。研究内容涵盖动物的空间定向、趋光、交配、竞争等行为表型及神经基础，也拓展至人类的注意、执行控制、工作记忆与决策等认知功能和决策机制。 课题组结合行为实验、计算建模、机器学习与计算机视觉方法，开展自动化行为侦测与定量分析，并进一步结合EEG、眼动、游戏化实验、数字媒体情境与用户研究，探索认知加工的动态脑机制及其应用。

本課題組以果蠅行為研究與人類認知任務研究為主要切入點，關注複雜環境中的感知編碼、行為選擇與決策機制。研究內容涵蓋動物的空間定向、趨光、交配、競爭等行為表型及神經基礎，也拓展至人類的注意、執行控制、工作記憶與決策等認知功能和決策機制。 課題組結合行為實驗、計算建模、機器學習與計算機視覺方法，開展自動化行為偵測與定量分析，並進一步結合EEG、眼動、遊戲化實驗、數字媒體情境與用戶研究，探索認知加工的動態腦機制及其應用。

Our group takes fruit-fly behavior and human cognitive tasks as major entry points to investigate perceptual encoding, behavioral choice, and decision-making in complex environments. Our research covers animal behavioral phenotypes and neural bases, including spatial orientation, phototaxis, mating, and competition, and also extends to human cognitive functions and decision mechanisms such as attention, executive control, working memory, and decision-making. By integrating behavioral experiments, computational modeling, machine learning, and computer vision, we develop automated behavioral detection and quantitative-analysis approaches, and further combine EEG, eye tracking, gamified experiments, digital-media contexts, and user research to explore the dynamic neural mechanisms of cognitive processing and their applications.
